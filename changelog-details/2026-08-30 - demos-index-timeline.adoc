= Demos index taxonomy and timeline

`/demos/` is reorganized around familiar UI domains and a dated accordion catalog.

* Categories are industry topics (Navigation, Windowing & spatial UI, Settings & context, …), not the pattern names themselves. Theme slot retitled *Theming & visual contracts*.
* Each catalog entry stores `releasedAt` and `modifiedAt` (ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`); backfilled from news, changelog, and git.
* Index UI: Released | Updated toggle (`localStorage` `hci-demos-date-mode`) sorts demos and categories by the chosen date and shows that date on each card.
* Categories render as native `<details>` accordions (multi-open; newest non-empty domain open by default; hash deep-links open the matching panel). Counts read as “3 demos” / “no demos yet” — bare TOC tallies removed.
* Homepage domains map updated to the new ids.
