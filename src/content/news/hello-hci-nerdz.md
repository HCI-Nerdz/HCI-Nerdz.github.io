HCI Nerdz is the home for **human–computer interaction** work that is graphical, spatial, and product-facing — UI chrome, discoverability, theme integrity, dialogs, desktop metaphors, and the cognitive honesty of what an interface teaches.

## Why this org exists

Software keeps spending human attention. Interfaces hide capability behind power-user syntax. Settings GUIs drift from the files they claim to represent. Previews fight the host theme. Filters advertise a shorter story than the search engine already knows.

Those are not polish nits. They are **product representation** bugs: the surface lies about what counts, what succeeded, and what the system can do.

## Sibling to OpenShellOrg

Shells and CLIs already have a home: [openshellorg](https://github.com/openshellorg). That org owns SOS, structured pipelines, progressive CLI help, and entrypoint honesty.

HCI Nerdz owns the other half of that fight — how humans *see and manipulate* systems.

## What ships next

Docs on [Antora + Valentus](https://hci-nerdz.github.io/docs/), this Astro site, and a migration path for UI-heavy repos currently parked under personal and sibling orgs.

Welcome. Keep your cognitive budget.
