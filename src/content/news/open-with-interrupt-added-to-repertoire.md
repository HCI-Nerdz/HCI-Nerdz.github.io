**Open-with interrupt** joins the published repertoire: a proposed first-class gesture that stops default file-handler handoff and asks which app should open *this* file *now*.

## What shipped

- An [essay](/blog/when-double-click-skips-the-choice/) on when double-click skips the choice
- A docs topic on the [Antora hub](https://hci-nerdz.github.io/docs/hci-nerdz/open-with-interrupt.html) with implementation paths (Windows AutoHotkey reference, macOS notes, Linux recipes)

## Why it belongs here

Stock desktops bury Open with in context menus and property sheets. Ctrl+double-click and middle-click on files are largely unused for that job — and map cleanly to “control the handler” / “alternate open.” The association list already knows multiple apps; the open gesture pretends otherwise.

That sits with product representation and intuition-over-backwards-convention — same neighborhood as [pass-through extensions](/news/pass-through-extensions-join-repertoire/).
